The bundled adblock/scriptlets.js contains adapted scriptlet behavior and small
implementation patterns from uBlock Origin's src/js/resources. Its copyright,
licence, and warranty notice are retained in that file. The complete upstream
licence is provided unchanged at META-INF/licenses/ublock-GPL-3.0.txt.

Source references under src/js/resources at that commit:
- set-constant.js: constant whitelist, type-change release, function stubs/cloaking.
- utils.js: missing-property traps and abort exception-token semantics.
- scriptlets.js: abortOnPropertyRead, abortOnPropertyWrite, noWindowOpenIf.
- abort-current-script.js: current-script checks and data-URL text decoding.
- prevent-addeventlistener.js: event-type and callback-source matching.
- prevent-settimeout.js: callback and delay matching, noop callback defusing.
- json-prune.js and object-prune.js: guarded JSON property-path pruning.
- prevent-fetch.js: request-property matching and synthetic responses.
